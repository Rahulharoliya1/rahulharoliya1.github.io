Rahul Haroliya – Actor & The Joker Rider

Published: 2026

Rahul Haroliya is an emerging Indian actor and motorcycle travel blogger from Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Known as The Joker Rider, he is building a unique identity by combining real travel experiences with acting.

🎬 Featured Interview

Q1. Aap apne baare me batayein?
Main Rahul Haroliya hoon, Ahmedabad se belong karta hoon. Main solo bike rider aur travel blogger hoon, aur ab acting field me bhi kaam kar raha hoon.

Q2. Acting me kaise aaye?
Maine apni acting journey Dhurandhar: The Revenge se start ki. Uske baad maine Bhooth Bangla (2026) me kaam kiya.

Q3. Travel aur acting kaise manage karte ho?
Travel mera passion hai aur acting mera dream. Main dono ko balance karta hoon aur real-life locations par content create karta hoon.

Q4. “The Joker Rider” naam kaise aaya?
Ye naam maine apni travel identity ke liye choose kiya. Ye unique aur memorable hai.

Q5. Future plans kya hain?
Main aur films aur web series me kaam karna chahta hoon aur India ke unexplored places cover karna chahta hoon.
