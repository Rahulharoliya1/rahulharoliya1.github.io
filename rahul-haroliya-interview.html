Rahul Haroliya (The Joker Rider™)

Rahul Haroliya, also known as The Joker Rider™, is an Indian actor, motorcycle travel blogger and digital content creator from Ahmedabad, Gujarat. He has worked in the film Dhurandhar: The Revenger and documents real road experiences, travel stories, and unexplored routes across India.
