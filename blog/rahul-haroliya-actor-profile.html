Rahul Haroliya – Indian Actor & Travel Blogger

Rahul Haroliya, also known as The Joker Rider, is an Indian background actor, motorcycle travel blogger and digital content creator based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Acting Career

Rahul Haroliya has worked as a background actor and crowd artist in film shooting projects in India. He has participated in large crowd scenes and film productions including shooting schedules in Mumbai.

He is actively working to build his acting profile in the film industry and continues to participate in film and media projects.

Travel Blogging – The Joker Rider

Besides acting, Rahul Haroliya is widely known online as The Joker Rider. Through his motorcycle journeys he documents real road experiences, travel stories and hidden destinations across India.

His blog shares practical travel guides, road trip experiences and offbeat locations for explorers.
