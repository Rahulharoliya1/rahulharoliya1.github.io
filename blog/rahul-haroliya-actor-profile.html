Rahul Haroliya – Indian Actor & Travel Blogger

Rahul Haroliya, also known as The Joker Rider, is an Indian background actor, motorcycle travel blogger and digital content creator based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Acting Career & Film Credits

Rahul Haroliya has worked as a background actor and crowd artist in major Indian film productions, including shooting schedules in Mumbai. His notable film associations include:

  • Hanuman Ansh – Background / Supporting Artist
  • Ramayana – Background / Crowd Artist
  • Dhurandhar: The Revenge – Uncredited Appearance (Protest Crowd Member)
  • Bhooth Bangla – Background Appearance

He is actively working to expand his acting portfolio in the Indian film and web series industry alongside his digital content creation.

Travel Blogging – The Joker Rider

Besides acting, Rahul Haroliya is widely known online as The Joker Rider. Through his motorcycle journeys, he documents real road experiences, travel stories, and unexplored destinations across India.

His blog shares practical travel guides, road trip strategies, and offbeat locations for adventure seekers and riders.
